I am a PhD Scholar at the Centre for Machine Intelligence and Data Science (CMInDS), Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. My work lies at the intersection of machine learning, statistics, financial risk, and data-driven decision making. I am currently interested in Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) and their applications in modeling complex relationships for problems in credit risk and financial systems.

<br>

Before joining IIT Bombay, I worked as a Business Analyst at Tata Consultancy Services, where I worked on model validation and credit risk related projects for global banking clients.

<br>

Outside research, I enjoy long-distance running, watching football in stadiums, travelling, and playing GeoGuessr.
