# Manish Dulal

I am a PhD Scholar at the Centre for Machine Intelligence and Data Science (CMInDS), Indian Institute of Technology Bombay. My work lies at the intersection of machine learning, statistics, financial risk, and data-driven decision making.

Before joining IIT Bombay, I worked as a Business Analyst at Tata Consultancy Services, where I worked on model validation and credit risk related projects for global banking clients. I also completed an internship at CRISIL, where I worked on Early Warning Systems for loan default prediction.

My research interests broadly include:

- Statistical Machine Learning
- Credit Risk Modelling
- Financial Data Science
- Artificial Intelligence
- Applied Statistics

Outside research, I enjoy long-distance running, football, and exploring analytical problems through data.
