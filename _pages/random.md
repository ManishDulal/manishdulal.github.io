## Football

Football has been a constant in my life since I was in Guwahati. I have been a member of the **Highlander Brigade** — the official supporters club of NorthEast United FC — since 2018. Those years meant regular trips to the Indira Gandhi Athletic Stadium in Sarusajai, chanting sessions, matchday activations, and banner-making as a club. I have also travelled to other stadiums to support the team on the road.

Beyond club football, I follow **Atlético Madrid** in La Liga and **Manchester United** in the Premier League. My favourite player is **Antoine Griezmann**.
