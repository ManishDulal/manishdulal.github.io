about me

PhD at IIT Bombay

I joined the PhD programme at the Centre for Machine Intelligence and Data Science (CMInDS), IIT Bombay in January 2025. I am advised by Prof. Usha Ananthakumar and co-advised by Dr. Piyush Pandey, both at the Shailesh J. Mehta School of Management.

My broad research interest is in understanding how machine learning methods — in particular graph neural networks and statistical learning approaches — can model complex dependency structures in financial systems. Credit risk is a natural domain for this: borrowers, institutions, and instruments are interconnected, and these relationships carry information that flat tabular models do not capture well.
